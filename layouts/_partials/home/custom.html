About us

Nills and Kelly are originally from Belgium and moved to the United States in 2018. Nills started running in 2015 for his health, ran his first trail race in 2017, and was hooked immediately — a first 50K followed in 2021, a first 100K in 2024, and a first 100-mile finish in 2025.

Kelly has lived with chronic pain since 2016. A 2022 hike through Joshua Tree showed her that movement helped her manage it, which eventually led to running — a first half marathon in 2023 and a first 50K in 2024. Running hasn't made the pain disappear, but moving has helped, and now it's something we share.
