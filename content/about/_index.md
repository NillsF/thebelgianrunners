## Different paths. Same trails.

We're Kelly and Nills, two Belgians living in California. We moved to the United States
in 2018 and found our way into running at different times, for different reasons.

### Nills: from a first run to 100 miles

Nills started running in 2015, mostly for his health. A first trail race in 2017
got him hooked on trails. From there it kept growing: a first 50K in 2021, a first
100K in 2024, and his first 100-mile finish in 2025.

### Kelly: finding her own way into running

Kelly has lived with chronic pain since 2016. In 2022, a hike through Joshua Tree
showed her that movement helped her manage her symptoms — running has not made her
pain disappear, but it's become part of how she keeps moving. That discovery led to a
first half marathon in 2023 and a first 50K in 2024.

### What we share now

These days we train together, race together
(usually somewhere in the middle of the pack), and share the whole thing online because
we genuinely enjoy it — the good runs, the bad ones, the gear that works, the gear that
doesn't, and everything in between.

We're middle-of-the-pack runners who take the sport seriously without taking
ourselves too seriously. Runners who happen to make content, not the other way around.
