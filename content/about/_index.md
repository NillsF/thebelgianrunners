## Two Belgians, two different roads into running

Nills and Kelly are originally from Belgium. They moved to the United States in 2018,
and somewhere along the way they both became runners — just not at the same time, and
not for the same reasons.

**Nills** started running in 2015, mostly for his health. A first trail race in 2017
changed everything — he was hooked on trails almost immediately. From there it kept
growing: a first 50K in 2021, a first 100K in 2024, and in 2025 he finally toed the
line for his first 100-mile race.

**Kelly** has lived with chronic pain since 2016. In 2022, a hike through Joshua Tree
showed her that movement helped her manage her symptoms — running has not made her
pain disappear, but it's become part of how she keeps moving. That discovery led to a
first half marathon in 2023 and a first 50K in 2024.

Different starting points, same trails. These days we train together, race together
(usually somewhere in the middle of the pack), and share the whole thing online because
we genuinely enjoy it — the good runs, the bad ones, the gear that works, the gear that
doesn't, and everything in between.

We're not coaches, we're not elite athletes, and we're not trying to sell you anything.
We're just two runners who happen to make content about it.
